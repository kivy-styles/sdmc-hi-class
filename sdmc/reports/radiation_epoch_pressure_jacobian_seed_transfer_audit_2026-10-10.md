## 1. Does gravity turn off for radiation?

No. In the units c=1, a flat perfect-fluid Friedmann model obeys
`H²=8πGρ/3`, `ä/a=−4πG(ρ+3p)/3`.
For an ideal radiation fluid `p=ρ/3`, the active acceleration source is `ρ+3p=2ρ`. Thus radiation pressure *contributes* to gravitational deceleration, although pressure gradients also oppose collapse of radiation overdensities.

The 4D Ricci **scalar** is `R=8πG(ρ−3p)=0` in this exact limit. But `R_{μν} R^{μν}=12H⁴` and the homogeneous Riemann-square `R_{μνρσ}R^{μνρσ}=24H⁴` are nonzero, whereas homogeneous Weyl-square and intrinsic spatial curvature vanish for flat FLRW. The Route-A term `F(σ)R` and the matter-window `W_m=4(p_struct−1)(2−p_struct)` are specifically trace-/matter-focused. Their radiation silence is a property of **those couplings**, not proof that radiation lacks gravity or that all scalar/triad sources must vanish.

An alternate radiation-era coupling would need to be derived from a pressure/enthalpy-sensitive geometric invariant or the radiation fluid action; `R_{μν}R^{μν}` is nonzero but **also** nonzero in matter domination, and a term sourcing the homogeneous mapper would spoil the selected background unless its variation vanishes on flat FLRW. No such coefficient or sector is supplied by the accepted frozen action.

## 4. Why radiation pressure is not by itself early galaxy collapse

In the tightly coupled photon-baryon plasma the sound speed is approximately `c/sqrt(3)`, and subhorizon radiation density perturbations oscillate under pressure support. Cold dark matter can grow slowly, approximately logarithmically following horizon entry in radiation domination. Therefore a radiation-era SDMC mechanism must be tested as **seed/transfer production for later CDM halos**, not as direct ordinary baryonic galaxy collapse. Radiation-era primordial black-hole collapse is possible only for unusual, large horizon-scale overdensities with pressure-threshold and abundance constraints; it is a *different* potential seed scenario, not a consequence of the homogeneous mapper.

A uniform linear halo-scale amplitude boost A=1.21 would raise the small-scale **matter power** by A²−1=46.4%, unlike the existing g3 nonlinear barrier model designed to preserve linear P(k). This is a stringent diagnostic for Ly-α, primordial transfer, CMB damping and other structure constraints (the numeric 46.4% is algebraic, **not** a measured limit). An environment-gated nonlinear source before matter domination faces the opposite problem: ordinary primordial perturbations have amplitudes near 10^-5, so cubic curvature/density sources are very small unless microphysics supplies a justified amplification.
