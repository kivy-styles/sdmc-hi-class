# SDMC Manuscript B, Part III — Retarded structural trace and Jordan metric response

## 434. Why the static trace elimination is not sufficient

The structural trace \ell has been retained as a propagating field, while
previous metric-response calculations eliminated it by setting all of
its time derivatives to zero. This procedure is correct only within
its physical validity window. The accepted cosmological background
uses the structural time t_s to define S and R_s=c t_s; the observable
time derivative below remains Jordan proper time t_c.

For each comoving Fourier mode in the reduced quadratic ADM supporting
sector, define

    u_k = delta ln(M)_k - Phi_J(k),       M=(S/S0)/a_J,
    q_k = u_k + ell_k/3,
    K=B f_L^2/3,
    omega0^2=m_L^2+c_L^2 k^2/a_J^2,
    C=4W eta_K k^4/a_s^4,               a_s=S/S0,
    Lambda=2C/(9K).

All real frequencies and couplings must be expressed in a consistent
Jordan proper-time unit system before observational use.

The reduced trace Euler–Lagrange equation, including time-dependent
kinetic coefficient K, is

    ddot ell + Gamma dot ell + omega_eff^2 ell = -3 Lambda u,
    Gamma=3H_J+dot K/K,
    omega_eff^2=omega0^2+Lambda.

This equation follows from the existing ADM structural trace action.
The driver u must ultimately be computed from the *coupled* Jordan
metric, active structural scalar and ADM lapse/shift constraints.

## 435. Causal trace susceptibility

Over an interval short compared with background variation, freeze the
coefficients K, omega0^2, Lambda, Gamma. For the Fourier convention

    u(t_c)=Re[U exp(-i omega t_c)],

the exact driven solution of the locally frozen equation is

    ell/U = -3 Lambda / D(omega,k),
    D = omega_eff^2 - omega^2 - i Gamma omega.

It follows directly that

    q/U = 1 + ell/(3U)
        = [omega0^2-omega^2-i Gamma omega]/D.

The metric/mapper response generated by varying the structural
curvature term at fixed trace is proportional to C q. Therefore the
local retarded response coefficient is

    K_ret(omega,k) = C [
        (omega0^2-omega^2-i Gamma omega) /
        (omega_eff^2-omega^2-i Gamma omega) ].

At omega=0 this gives precisely the previously implemented static
Schur complement:

    K_ret(0,k) = C omega0^2/(omega0^2+Lambda) = K_geom(k).

At frequencies much higher than omega_eff:

    ell/U -> 0, q/U ->1, K_ret -> C.

The structural trace cannot adjust rapidly, so the effective
instantaneous metric response is generally different from the
static equilibrium response.

For Gamma>0, the retarded mode poles lie in the lower
frequency half-plane. For sufficiently small damping, the response
has a resonance near omega_eff. For Gamma=0 an exactly driven
resonance has no finite steady response; do not treat that pole as
a valid static correction.

This local **frequency response is not an instantaneous covariant
action term** at nonzero omega. Integrating out a dynamical degree of
freedom gives a retarded memory kernel, and a time-nonlocal influence
on the metric/structural mapper. A complex frequency-domain response
does not imply a negative physical energy merely because its real
part can change sign.

## 436. Time-domain memory and switching on a structural well

With constant coefficients and Gamma>=0, the retarded Green function
for an underdamped trace mode is

    G_R(t) = Theta(t) exp(-Gamma t/2) sin(Omega t)/Omega,
    Omega^2=omega_eff^2-Gamma^2/4.

For vanishing trace initial conditions and arbitrary driver u(t),

    ell(t) = -3 Lambda int_0^t G_R(t-t') u(t') dt'.

If u jumps from zero to constant u0 at t=0, its exact solution is

    ell(t) = ell_static * [
       1-exp(-Gamma t/2)
         (cos(Omega t) + Gamma/(2Omega) sin(Omega t))],

    ell_static=-3 Lambda u0/omega_eff^2.

This provides a direct numerical comparison between the action-derived
differential equation and the analytic causal solution. An expanding
structural well need not remain at its static equilibrium value if
its driver evolves on comparable or faster timescales.

For time-dependent coefficients, use the original differential
equation; the frozen Fourier/Green functions are local approximations.
The earlier energy-exchange identity remains valid for the exact
time-varying reduced equations.

## 437. Constraint coupling without inventing the rolling G3 block

The *complete* classical SDMC action has

    G2=k1(sigma)Z+k2(sigma)Z^2-V(sigma),
    G3=g(sigma)Z,       G4=F(sigma)/2,

and its accepted rolling No-Slip background obeys alpha_B=-2alpha_M,
with the action-level identity D=K_cov-6alpha_M+6alpha_M^2.

Let H0(omega,k,a) denote the **correctly gauge-fixed and constrained**
Jordan metric/structural scalar response operator of that full
rolling action. It is not derived or assumed in this branch. Its
physical scalar variables can include Psi_J, Phi_J and delta sigma,
but the ADM lapse/shift must be removed consistently.

After eliminating the structural trace, let u=v^T x for the remaining
physical scalar variables x. In a locally frozen frequency-domain
linear-response problem the structural term contributes

    H_full = H0 + lambda v v^T,
    lambda = -2 K_ret(omega,k)

for the same signed Jordan quadratic-Lagrangian convention as the
previous non-braided covariant checkpoint.

If x0=H0^-1 b, y=H0^-1 v and the inverse is nonsingular, then

    x = x0 - lambda y (v^T x0)/(1+lambda v^T y).

This is the exact algebraic Sherman–Morrison/Schur response.
No empirical gravitational coupling is inserted.

The rank-one expression is valid for any independently derived
nonsingular symmetric H0, including an indefinite gravitational
constraint Hessian; a complex symmetric *retarded* operator is
allowed as a frequency-domain diagnostic. For a singular gauge or
constraint block, do not use a pseudoinverse and claim a solved
cosmology.

At omega=0, supplying the signed non-braided
F R/2 + k1 Z -V Hessian recovers the previous static structural
constraint matrix *exactly*. The new tests verify that equality.

The full rolling G3, k2Z^2 and evolving F sector will also have
frequency-dependent mixing with the Jordan metric. Combining a
retarded trace susceptibility with a *static* non-braided H0 is only
a mixed-order algebraic check, not a complete dynamical prediction.

## 437a. No-Slip of the scalar sector is not automatically No-Slip of all geometry

The accepted rolling Horndeski background uses alpha_B=-2alpha_M,
which leads to equal Newtonian and Weyl gravitational strengths in
the no-support scalar-only theory under its usual assumptions.

With supporting-space perturbations, however, the rank-one update
changes the metric observables differently in general.

Use the physical metric projection l^T x=Phi_J-Psi_J, with l having
+1 on Phi and -1 on Psi. From the exact rank-one operator update,

    slip_new = slip_0 - [
         lambda (l^T H0^-1 v)(v^T H0^-1 b)
         / (1+lambda v^T H0^-1 v)
    ].

Thus a no-slip baseline slip_0=0 can acquire a nonzero geometric slip,
unless the structural excitation has no source projection
(v^T H0^-1 b=0), no slip projection
(l^T H0^-1 v=0), or an exactly zero geometric kernel.

This is purely exact linear algebra applied to the action-derived
structural response. It does not assert that the accepted SDMC
full action necessarily produces slip. Once H0 is actually derived
from rolling G3 and its ADM constraints, compute both:

    Newtonian response Psi_J
    Weyl response (Phi_J+Psi_J)/2

rather than inheriting a scalar-only No-Slip equality.

The function projected_metric_response and three new regression tests
verify (i) a conditional slip induced from an initially no-slip
baseline, (ii) aligned geometric coupling that preserves no slip,
and (iii) the complex finite-frequency metric projections against
direct inversion.

## 438. Why static gravity can fail at finite frequency

Necessary but not sufficient conditions for the trace equilibrium
approximation include

    |omega|/omega_eff << 1,
    |Gamma omega|/omega_eff^2 << 1,

plus small fractional rates of variation of K, omega0^2, C and
the structural clock compared with the trace response rate.
A large omega_eff/H_J ratio alone does not ensure these conditions:
the relevant metric/mapper driver can oscillate near omega_eff.

The implementation computes both the exact local response and a
scaled discrepancy from the static kernel. No physical wave number
in h/Mpc can be assigned without deriving the action coefficient
units and a consistent structural-to-Jordan mapping.

## 438a. Independent trace-sector anti-damping condition

The reduced homogeneous trace has

    ell_ddot + Gamma ell_dot + omega_eff² ell = 0

for fixed or locally frozen coefficients. Its local characteristic
growth rates are

    lambda± = [-Gamma ± sqrt(Gamma²-4 omega_eff²)]/2.

For a positive restoring frequency omega_eff²>0:
- Gamma>0 yields damped local homogeneous trace dynamics;
- Gamma=0 yields marginal undamped oscillations;
- Gamma<0 yields an anti-damped local trace mode, even though
  K>0 and omega_eff²>0.

In an expanding Jordan epoch H_J>0,

    Gamma=3H_J+dot K/K,

therefore a **necessary local no-growth bound** is

    d ln K/d ln a_J >= -3,
    K=B f_L²/3.

Equivalently, the combined coefficient B f_L² must not fall
faster than a_J^-3 over the frozen local interval. For example,
dot K/K=-4H_J gives Gamma=-H_J and a homogeneous local growth
rate +H_J/2 when underdamped.

This does not replace the ghost, scalar sound-speed, full
Horndeski-ADM eigenmode and background checks. It is a new
independent structural trace damping check.

The function local_trace_stability tests these roots, with
separate numerical checks of damping, marginality and anti-damping.

## 439. Interpretation and remaining test

The current result establishes from the reduced structural action:
(1) its causal response memory;
(2) its local structural-trace resonance scale and damping;
(3) its static and high-frequency limiting kernel;
(4) its exact rank-one algebraic coupling to a supplied Jordan
constraint operator.

It does NOT establish:
- an action-derived physical homogeneous rho_g,
- separate conservation of that geometric density,
- a pressureless geometric attractor,
- the rolling G3 + lapse/shift constrained propagator H0,
- physical observational mu(k,a), Sigma(k,a),
- CMB, BAO or weak-lensing likelihoods without CDM.

The material inventory M_u remains distinct from the effective
geometric response and from the observable M_inferred.
